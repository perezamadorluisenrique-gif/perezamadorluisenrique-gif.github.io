Linkvane Pro

Linkvane finds the broken links on your site. Pro fixes them: change a dead or redirected link in every post at once, turn 404s into redirects, and get told when a link on a page that matters stops working.

What it does and who it is for

It is for blogs that earn from affiliate links, and for freelancers and agencies who keep other people's sites healthy. The free Linkvane plugin scans and reports. Linkvane Pro is the part that saves you the hours of editing by hand.

What Pro adds

  • One-click fixes, in batchesWhen a link redirects, change it to the final address in every post at once. When an external link is dead, link to its archived copy (the Internet Archive's public API) or remove the link and keep the text. Every change can be undone.
  • 404 log to redirectsSee which missing URLs visitors actually hit, accept a suggested destination, and create the 301. Loops (A to B to A) are refused.
  • Watched pages and alertsMark the pages that matter (home, sales, affiliate pages). Get an email or webhook (JSON or Slack) when a link on them breaks, or when a page that answers "200" says the product is no longer available.
  • Review queue and diff previewSee what will change before it changes, untick single posts, and unlink instead of replace.
  • Client reportsA report with your own name and logo, as printable page and CSV, and a monthly email.
  • WP-CLI and multisiteRun fixes, redirects and reports from the command line, and see a network dashboard.

What stays free, for good

  • Scanning, the list of broken links, the dashboard and the SEO audit in Linkvane.
  • Seeing where each broken link is and editing it yourself.
  • Redirects you create keep working even if your Pro licence ends.

Nothing that is free in Linkvane is moved into Pro.

How you get it and install it

  1. After you buy, the payment service sends you a zip file and your licence key.
  2. In WordPress open Plugins > Add New Plugin > Upload Plugin, choose the zip (do not unzip it) and activate it. The free Linkvane plugin must be installed first.
  3. Open Tools > Linkvane Pro License, paste the key and press Activate.

It is not in the WordPress.org directory, so WordPress will not offer updates for it by itself. In Tools > Linkvane Pro License, press "Check for update" and, if there is a newer version, download it again from your purchase email and upload it over the old one. Your settings and redirects stay.

Needs WordPress 6.2 or newer, PHP 8.0 or newer and Linkvane 1.7.2 or newer.

Network and privacy

  • Links are checked from your own server. Nothing about your site is sent to us.
  • The only requests to a third party are the ones you ask for: an archived copy of a dead link (one URL sent to the Internet Archive), and the webhook address you type in.
  • The "Check for update" button reads one small public file with version numbers, only when you press it.
  • No telemetry, no analytics. This page has no cookies and loads nothing from other sites.

Questions

Does it change my posts without asking?

No. Every fix shows a preview first, you choose which posts to change, and every change can be undone.

What happens if my licence ends?

Everything you already created keeps working, and redirects keep being served. You just cannot create new fixes or redirects until you renew.

Can I use it on my clients' sites?

Yes, on sites you own or manage. You may not resell or share the file or the key.

Does it work with page builders?

Posts built with Elementor or Divi are skipped and flagged, so nothing is broken by editing their stored layout.
