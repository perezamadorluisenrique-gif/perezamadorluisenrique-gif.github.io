What it is and who it is for

Thesis and Report Vault is an Obsidian vault, not a plugin. It gives students, researchers and engineers a structure that is tedious to build: a thesis manifest, chapter and appendix templates, a single-note report in the style of a standard, and templates for literature notes and supervisor meetings.

It is made for the free Section Numbering plugin, which numbers headings (1, 1.1, 1.1.1) and keeps a live table of contents. Examples are included in English and in Spanish.

What is inside

  • TemplatesThesis chapter, appendix, report, literature note and supervisor meeting, in English and in Spanish.
  • A worked thesis exampleA short original thesis with abstract, introduction, literature review, method, results, discussion, conclusion, references and two appendices, numbered with the free plugin. The Spanish version sits beside it. Data and references in the examples are fictional and marked as such.
  • A technical report exampleA single-note report with scope, references, terms, requirements and an annex, in English and in Spanish.
  • Literature notesTwo example reading notes with the fields to fill: authors, year, source, key claims, quotes with page and how you will use it.
  • A numbering guideHow numbering works, what the free plugin does, what Pro adds, and what to do when you reorder chapters.

What you need

  • Obsidian, and the free Section Numbering plugin from the community directory. The pack works with the free plugin: chapters are numbered and the table of contents updates without paying for anything.
  • Section Numbering Pro is optional. It adds numbering across the whole document, profiles, cross-references, figure and table numbers and export. The guide explains where it helps. Nothing in the pack depends on it.

What you get: one zip of Markdown files and images. No plugin code, no installer, no activation.

How to use it

  1. Download the zip from the receipt email or the payment page.
  2. Unzip it anywhere you like.
  3. In Obsidian choose Open folder as vault and pick the unzipped folder.
  4. Follow Start here: install Section Numbering, open the example thesis, then copy a template and start writing.

Privacy

Questions

Do I need Section Numbering Pro?

No. The chapters in the pack are numbered with the free plugin.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, the vault is plain Markdown and opens in Obsidian mobile.

Is it tied to a university format?

No. It is a general academic structure. Check your institution's rules and adjust the headings.

Will I get updates?

Improvements to the pack will be available from the same payment page. Check it for the current version.

Does it manage citations?

No. It has literature notes and a references page you fill in. It does not generate bibliographies.
