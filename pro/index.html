How the Pro plugins work

  • Pro plugins are closed-source software under a commercial licence, not in the Obsidian community directory. After you buy, the Bolsterfy shop sends a download link and a licence key: install the zip, paste the key in the plugin settings and press Activate.
  • No telemetry, works offline. Activate sends the key once to the Bolsterfy shop's licence endpoint.
  • Refunds follow the policy of the Bolsterfy shop, shown before you pay.
