Pro plugins for Obsidian

Three Pro versions of Obsidian plugins. One-time payment, no subscription. What was already free stays free for good.

  • Book Studio Pro

    Turn your Markdown notes into a paperback Amazon KDP accepts and an accessible EPUB, with the finishing tools a published book needs.

    59 USD one-time payment

  • Section Numbering Pro

    Number documents, not just headings: contracts, theses, standards, manuals and books that span several notes and cite each other.

    15 USD one-time payment · 14-day free trial

  • Update Radar Pro

    Make plugin updates safe: a copy before every update, a way back to how everything was, and a guided search for the plugin that breaks something.

    9 USD one-time payment · 14-day free trial

How it works

  • Buy a licence key, receive it in the receipt email, paste it in the plugin settings and press Activate.
  • Open source, no telemetry, works offline. Activate sends the key once to the payment service's licence endpoint.
  • Refunds are handled by the payment service.
