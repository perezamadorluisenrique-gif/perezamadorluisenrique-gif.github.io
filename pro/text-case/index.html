What it does and who it is for

Text Format changes case and cleans up the selection or the current note. Pro is for the day you need to clean many notes the same way: it saves your steps as recipes, runs them over a folder or the vault with a preview and one-step undo, and cleans pasted text according to where it came from.

It works best with the free Text Format plugin installed, and needs Obsidian 1.7.2 or later.

What Pro adds

  • RecipesNamed chains of cleanups, case changes and your saved replacements, each with its own command and hotkey.
  • Clean a folder or the whole vaultA preview of every note with a tick box per note; nothing changes until you confirm.
  • Undo the whole batchOne command puts every note back; a note you edited since is never overwritten.
  • Report of what changedA note listing what each step changed in each note, with links.
  • Paste profilesPDF, web page or email: hyphens and wrapped lines rejoined, reply quoting and signatures removed.
  • Rejoin hyphenated words with exceptionsinfor- / mation becomes information, while self- / service stays self-service.

What stays free, for good

  • Case commands and identifier styles (camelCase, snake_case, slug and more).
  • Tidy note and clean on paste.
  • Saved find-and-replace commands.
  • Sort, dedupe and remove blank lines.

Nothing in the free plugin moves into Pro.

Questions

Do I need the free plugin?

Pro works best with the free Text Format plugin installed. The free plugin stays free and keeps doing what it does today.

Can I undo a batch?

Yes. One command puts every note of the batch back. A note you edited since is never overwritten.

Does it change my notes without asking?

No. Cleaning a folder or the vault shows a preview with a tick box per note, and nothing changes until you confirm.

What do I get for the price?

The plugin for good, for your own use, plus one year of updates. After that it keeps working; you can renew for half the price to keep getting updates.
