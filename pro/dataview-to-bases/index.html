What it does and who it is for

Dataview to Bases converts Dataview queries into Obsidian Bases one note at a time. Pro is for vaults with dozens or hundreds of queries: it moves the whole vault with a preview and one-step undo, and then runs every converted query in Dataview and in Bases to show you the ones that list different notes.

It needs the free Dataview to Bases plugin and Obsidian 1.10 or later.

What Pro adds

  • Convert the whole vault at onceA preview of every note with a tick box per note; nothing changes until you confirm.
  • Undo the whole batchOne command puts every note back; a note you edited since is never overwritten.
  • Shared .base filesA query used in two notes or more becomes one .base file, embedded where it was.
  • Simple dataviewjsdv.table() and dv.list() over dv.pages() with where, sort and limit are converted too.
  • Migration reportA note with a link to every converted note and the reason for every query left alone.
  • Check against DataviewEach converted query runs in Dataview and in Bases; the report lists the ones whose notes differ.

What stays free, for good

  • Convert the query at the cursor or every query in a note.
  • Vault report: what converts, what partly and what not, with reasons.
  • Move inline fields (key:: value) to properties, with undo.
  • The original query is kept under each converted block.

Nothing in the free plugin moves into Pro.

Questions

Do I need the free plugin?

Yes. Dataview to Bases Pro needs the free Dataview to Bases plugin. The free plugin stays free and keeps converting one note at a time.

Can I undo a batch?

Yes. One command puts every note of the batch back. A note you edited since is never overwritten.

Will every query convert?

No. Some queries have no equivalent in Bases. The migration report lists every query that was left alone and the reason.

What do I get for the price?

The plugin for good, for your own use, plus one year of updates. After that it keeps working; you can renew for half the price to keep getting updates.
