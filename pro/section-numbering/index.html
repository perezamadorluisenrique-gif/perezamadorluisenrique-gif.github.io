What it does and who it is for

The free Section Numbering plugin numbers the headings of a note. Pro is for people who write documents that live in several notes, cite each other ("see art. 3.2(b)") and leave Obsidian for Word, Pandoc or LaTeX: lawyers, researchers, engineers and authors.

It requires the free Section Numbering plugin (0.10.0 or later) and works with it.

What Pro adds

  • Numbering profilesEach heading level gets its own template: Título I. / Artículo 7. / 7.1 / (a). Built in: legal-es, legal-en, contrato, academico, iso, libro and outline. Write your own as JSON.
  • Cross-references between notesA reference is an ordinary link whose alias carries the heading's current number, and it follows when the target is renumbered. An alias you wrote yourself is never touched.
  • Documents made of many notesA manifest note lists the notes in order, and the whole document is numbered as one text, with a master index and a compiled export.
  • Figures, tables and listingsNumbered per chapter, referenced like headings, with lists of figures, tables and listings.
  • Structure panelThe numbered outline with how many links point at each section, one-click fixes for level jumps, duplicate or empty headings and stale references, and buttons to move or demote a section. Links update in the same edit.
  • ExportNumbered Markdown, an HTML page, Markdown for Pandoc, LaTeX, and copy as rich text to paste into Word or an email.
  • RulesChoose a profile per note, per document, per tag or per folder, and ask the plugin to explain which rule decided.

What stays free, for good

  • Outline numbers (1., 1.1., 1.2.) for your headings, written into the note or only shown on screen.
  • Links to renumbered headings keep working.
  • A table of contents that keeps itself up to date.
  • Interface in nine languages.

Nothing in the free plugin moves into Pro. After the trial ends, numbers already in your notes keep working and Flatten to plain numbers always works; writing profile numbers and exporting need a key.

Network and privacy

Questions

Do I need the free plugin?

Yes. Section Numbering Pro requires the free Section Numbering plugin (0.10.0 or later).

What happens when the trial ends?

What is already in your notes keeps working, and Flatten to plain numbers always works. Writing numbers with a profile and exporting need a key.

Will it change my notes behind my back?

No. Numbers are written only when you run a command, and one Ctrl+Z undoes a numbering, links included.

Where do I find my key?

In the receipt email from the payment service.
