What it does and who it is for

Typography as You Type fixes quotes, dashes and ellipses as you type. Pro is for people who write in more than one language or who publish their notes: it gives each folder or note its own style (quotes, dashes, French spacing), composes whole folders or the vault at once, and checks everything before it goes out.

It works best with the free Typography as You Type plugin installed, and needs Obsidian 1.7.2 or later.

What Pro adds

  • Typography profilesen-us, en-gb, es, fr and de set quotes, dash style and French spacing together, per note, per folder, by language or as a default.
  • French spacingNarrow no-break space before ; ! ?, no-break space before : and inside guillemets, also as you type.
  • Compose a folder or the whole vaultA preview of every change with a tick box per note; nothing changes until you confirm.
  • Undo the whole batchOne command puts every note back; a note you edited since is never overwritten.
  • Inconsistency checkStraight quotes, marks from another style, unmatched quotes, double spaces, mixed dashes and missing French spaces, each with a link to its line.

What stays free, for good

  • Curly quotes, dashes, ellipsis and arrows as you type, never in code, math or links.
  • Quotation marks per note, per folder or by the lang property.
  • Apply typography to the selection or the current note.

Nothing in the free plugin moves into Pro.

How to activate

  1. After you buy, the Bolsterfy shop emails you a download link for the zip file and your licence key.
  2. Install the zip following the INSTALL file inside it, then open the plugin's settings in Obsidian and paste the key.
  3. Press Activate.

Questions

Do I need the free plugin?

Pro works best with the free Typography as You Type plugin installed. The free plugin stays free and keeps doing what it does today.

Can I undo a batch?

Yes. One command puts every note of the batch back. A note you edited since is never overwritten.

Does it change my notes without asking?

No. Composing a folder or the vault shows a preview with a tick box per note, and nothing changes until you confirm.

What do I get for the price?

The plugin for good, for your own use, plus one year of updates. After that it keeps working; you can renew for half the price to keep getting updates.
