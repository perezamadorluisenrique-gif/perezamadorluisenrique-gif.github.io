What Book Studio does

Turn a folder of Markdown notes into a print-ready paperback PDF for Amazon KDP and an EPUB 3 for every ebook store. Everything happens on your device: your manuscript is never uploaded anywhere.

How to switch from Better Export PDF to Book Studio

  1. Install Book Studio from Install from the Obsidian directory.
  2. In Settings, Community plugins, turn Better Export PDF off without deleting it, so the two do not act on the same action at once.
  3. Open the Book Studio settings and review them once: settings from Better Export PDF are not copied over.
  4. When everything works the way you expect, uninstall Better Export PDF.
