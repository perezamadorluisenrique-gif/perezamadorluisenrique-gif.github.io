What Dice Rolls does

Roll dice right inside your notes. Write a formula in inline code that starts with dice: and it turns into its result. Click the result to roll again.

How to switch from Dice Roller to Dice Rolls

  1. Install Dice Rolls from Install from the Obsidian directory.
  2. In Settings, Community plugins, turn Dice Roller off without deleting it, so the two do not act on the same action at once.
  3. Open the Dice Rolls settings and review them once: settings from Dice Roller are not copied over.
  4. When everything works the way you expect, uninstall Dice Roller.
