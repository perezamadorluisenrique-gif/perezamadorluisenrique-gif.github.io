What Word Lookup does

Look up any word in a sidebar dictionary: definitions, phonetics with audio, examples, and synonyms and antonyms you can click to look up in turn. Replace a word with a synonym from the right-click menu, or make a note for it.

How to switch from Dictionary to Word Lookup

  1. Install Word Lookup from Install from the Obsidian directory.
  2. In Settings, Community plugins, turn Dictionary off without deleting it, so the two do not act on the same action at once.
  3. Open the Word Lookup settings and review them once: settings from Dictionary are not copied over.
  4. When everything works the way you expect, uninstall Dictionary.
