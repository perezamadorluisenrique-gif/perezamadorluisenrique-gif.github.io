Book Studio for the web

Turn Markdown notes into a KDP print PDF and an accessible EPUB 3, in your browser, with nothing uploaded.

Open the web app Open in your browser Source code and issues

What it does

Book Studio for the web is the browser version of the Book Studio plugin. Open a folder of Markdown notes, or write and import a manuscript (.md, .txt or Word .docx), and export a print-ready PDF interior for Amazon KDP and an EPUB 3 that passes EPUBCheck.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account, no cookies and no analytics. It is free and open source.

Highlights

  • Print PDF with the inside margin KDP requires for your page count, embedded fonts and optional bleed.
  • Accessible EPUB 3 with a navigable table of contents, the book's language and image descriptions.
  • Knuth–Plass line breaking, hyphenation in six languages, drop caps, running heads and chapter openers.
  • A chapter editor, drag-to-reorder chapter list, book details form and front cover, all saved in your browser.
  • Imports .md, .txt and .docx manuscripts and lists what it left out.
  • A free publish checklist and a live paged preview that lists broken links and missing images.
