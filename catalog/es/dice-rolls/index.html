Plugin de Obsidian gratis

Dice Rolls

Inline dice rolls you click to reroll, in Live Preview and Reading view, with full dice notation, a roll dialog and a roll history. Reads Dice Roller notes.

Instalar desde el directorio de Obsidian Abrir en Obsidian Código fuente e incidencias

Qué hace

El texto que describe el producto está en inglés, igual que en su repositorio.

Roll dice right inside your notes. Write a formula in inline code that starts with dice: and it turns into its result. Click the result to roll again.

It works in Live Preview and in Reading view, on desktop and on mobile. Hover over a result to see the formula and every die rolled. Put the cursor inside a roll to edit its formula. A roll keeps its result while you edit around it; it changes only when you click it.

Lo más destacado

  • No surprise rewrites of your notes. A dice-mod: roll writes its result into the note only when you click it, or when you run Write the results of dice-mod rolls in this note. Either way it is one change that one undo takes back, and it is refused if the note changed in the meantime.
  • Custom ranges include both ends. 1d[3,5] rolls 3, 4 or 5, and so does 1d[3-5]. A list of three or more numbers, such as 1d[1,3,5], is a die with exactly those faces.
  • A roll history in the sidebar, with the note each roll came from, and a dialog to roll any formula without writing it into a note.
  • Clear errors. A formula that cannot be rolled stays visible, underlined, with the reason in its tooltip.
