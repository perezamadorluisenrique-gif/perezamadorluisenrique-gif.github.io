List Item Callouts

Highlight single list items as coloured callouts by starting them with a character such as & ! ? or @. Compatible with List Callouts notes.

Instalar desde el directorio de Obsidian Abrir en Obsidian Código fuente e incidencias

Qué hace

El texto que describe el producto está en inglés, igual que en su repositorio.

Highlight a single list item as a coloured callout by starting it with a character and a space:

It works in Live Preview, in Source mode and in Reading view, on desktop and on mobile, in bullet, numbered and task lists.

Lo más destacado

  • Long notes keep their colours. List Callouts stops drawing callouts when Obsidian has not finished reading a long note (#75, #80). This plugin reads the lines on screen itself, so it never gives up.
  • Wrapped lines are coloured with the item they belong to (#48, #18), and in Reading view a callout item's sub-list sits inside its colour.
  • Commands to add, change and remove a callout (#47, #57, #71), each with an icon for the mobile toolbar.
  • Names for callouts, shown in the picker, so you remember what ~ was for.
