Free Obsidian plugin

Section Numbering

Numbers your headings as an outline (1, 1.1, 1.2), shown on screen without changing the note or written into it, with a live table of contents. Links to renumbered headings keep working.

Install from the Obsidian directory Open in Obsidian Source code and issues

What it does

Outline numbers for your headings — 1., 1.1., 1.2. — that stay right when you move sections around, without breaking the links that point at them. Show the numbers on screen and leave your notes untouched, or write them into the text; add a table of contents that keeps itself up to date.

Highlights

  • wikilinks and embeds, [[Guide#Setup|alias]] and ![[Guide#Setup]], keeping the alias;
  • Markdown links, text, encoded or in angle brackets;
  • nested heading paths, [[Guide#Method#Setup]];
  • wikilinks inside properties;
  • links within the note itself and, unless you switch it off, in every other note that links to it.
