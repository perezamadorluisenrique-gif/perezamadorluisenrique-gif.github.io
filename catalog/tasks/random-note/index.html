How to choose

Check when each plugin was last updated, whether its description covers exactly what you need, and whether it supports your version of Obsidian. Downloads tell you how many people installed a plugin, not which one works better. Try one at a time on a copy of your vault.

Where these facts come from

Name, author, description, version, last-update date and downloads come from the public Obsidian directory files (community-plugins.json and community-plugin-stats.json), read on 24 November 2026. Download counts are the ones Obsidian publishes.

This page is written by the person who makes Random Note Filters. If you maintain one of the listed plugins and want something corrected or removed, open an issue in the repository and it will be handled.
