Hidden Problems Lab

Three free apps for problems people don't notice until they hurt: a refill that suddenly looks different, a recalled product still in the house, and website certificates that will start expiring much faster.

Tres aplicaciones gratuitas para problemas que la gente no nota a tiempo.

  • Same Pill, New Look

    Warns you when a refill comes from a different generic maker and will look different, so you check with your pharmacist instead of skipping doses. English and Spanish.

    Le avisa cuando su resurtido viene de otro fabricante y se verá diferente, para que lo confirme con su farmacéutico.

    It never identifies a pill. A new look means a new maker: confirm with your pharmacist.

  • RecallRadar

    Checks the products, food and car you already own against U.S. recalls from CPSC, the FDA and NHTSA, then links the official notice for the refund, replacement or repair.

    Revisa si los productos, alimentos y el auto que ya tiene están bajo un retiro del mercado en EE. UU.

  • 47-Day Cert Radar

    Enter a domain to see every public TLS certificate from Certificate Transparency logs and when each one breaks as maximum lifetimes drop to 200, 100 and then 47 days.

    Muestra los certificados TLS públicos de un dominio y cuándo fallarán con los nuevos plazos más cortos.

Private by design

No accounts and no servers. What you enter stays in your browser, and lookups go straight from your browser to the public sources each app names. Visits are counted with GoatCounter: no cookies, no personal data, only the page path, the referring site and the screen width, and nothing when Do Not Track is on.

Sin cuentas ni servidores. Sus datos se quedan en su dispositivo.

Free and open source: Same Pill, New Look · RecallRadar · 47-Day Cert Radar. Found a problem? Open an issue on the app's repository.
